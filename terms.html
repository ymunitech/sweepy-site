Sweepy: Photo & Storage Cleaner for iPhone · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Important notice

Sweepy helps you find and remove photos, videos and contacts from your own iPhone. You decide what is deleted. Items you choose go to the Basket first, and iOS asks for a final confirmation before photos and videos are moved to Recently Deleted, where Apple keeps them for 30 days. Contacts are backed up inside the app before any merge or deletion. Please review the Basket before you confirm: we cannot recover anything you delete after those safeguards.

Sweepy is currently free and has no account. We plan to introduce an auto-renewing subscription, Sweepy Pro, in a later version; section 10 sets out the terms that will apply to it. The App may also send anonymous usage statistics to our analytics provider, as described in section 12 and in the Privacy Policy.

5. Cleaning, deletion and recovery

Sweepy's groups, suggestions, "Keep" marks and Smart Cleaning picks are produced by automated analysis and may contain mistakes: two different photos may be grouped as similar, or the frame you prefer may not be the one marked. They are suggestions, not decisions. Before confirming, review the Basket and return anything you want to keep.

When you confirm a deletion, iOS moves photos and videos to the Recently Deleted album of the Photos app, where Apple keeps them for about 30 days before permanent removal; you can restore them from there during that period. Once that period has passed, or if you empty Recently Deleted yourself, the items cannot be recovered by us or by Apple. If iCloud Photos is enabled, deletions sync to all your devices.

6. Contacts

Duplicate groups, similar-name matches and merge previews are suggestions. Before Sweepy merges or deletes contacts it saves a safety copy inside the App; you can restore it from Contacts › Restore and undo the most recent merge. Safety copies live only on your device and are removed when you delete the App, so export a backup (.vcf) if you want a copy elsewhere. If your contacts sync with iCloud, Google or Exchange, changes made in Sweepy propagate to those accounts under their rules.

7. Compression, conversion and Secret Library

Video Compress and Photo Compress create a smaller copy (HEVC or HEIC) of the item you choose and, only after that copy has been saved successfully, remove the original. Compression reduces quality; review the result before confirming. Live to Still keeps the photo and removes the attached video clip; this cannot be reversed.

Secret Library stores the items you move there inside the App, protected by a PIN that you choose. The PIN cannot be recovered or reset by us; if you forget it, the only way to use the feature again is to delete and reinstall the App, which removes the Secret Library and everything in it. Items in the Secret Library are not part of Apple Photos and are not backed up by iCloud Photos; they are included in a device backup only if you make one. Export anything important back to Photos before deleting the App.
