Clean safely, keep what matters

Sweepy frees up space on your iPhone: duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos, large videos and messy contacts — reviewed by you, deleted only when you confirm.

Download on the App Store

Nothing is deleted right away

Everything goes to the Basket first. Check it, return what you want, then delete. Photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days after that.

Smart Cleaning

One tap picks the extra copies in every group and keeps the best shot — open eyes, best faces, sharpest frame.

Swipe

Go through a month like a deck of cards: left to clean, right to keep, undo any time.

Phone Health

A score for your iPhone with storage, memory, heat and CPU, and a forecast of when space may run out.

Contacts

Duplicates, similar names, entries without a name or number — with a safety copy before every change.

Private by design

No account, no uploads, no ads. Photos and contacts never leave your device; only anonymous usage statistics do, and you can switch those off.
